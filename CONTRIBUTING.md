# Contributing to Empty405 Lab

Empty405 Lab is an open space for criticism, research, experiments, and collaborative exploration.

You do not need to agree with an idea to contribute.

Useful contributions include:

- identifying incorrect assumptions;
- providing relevant research or prior art;
- proposing alternative architectures;
- improving technical accuracy;
- reproducing or challenging experiments;
- building proofs of concept;
- identifying security, ethical, economic, or engineering limitations;
- improving documentation.

## Research First

Most material in `research/` should be treated as work under investigation.

A proposal is not automatically a specification.

A hypothesis is not a fact.

An AI-generated explanation is not evidence by itself.

When possible, contributions should distinguish between evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, and speculation.

## Issues

Use Issues to report:

- factual or technical errors;
- missing prior work;
- implementation problems;
- research questions;
- proposed experiments;
- improvements to existing material.

## Contributions

Pull requests are welcome.

For substantial changes, opening an Issue first is encouraged so the idea can be discussed before significant work is done.

## Experiments

Negative results are valuable.

If an experiment demonstrates that an idea does not work, that result should be documented rather than hidden.

The purpose of the lab is not to prove every idea correct.

The purpose is to discover what survives testing.

## Collaboration

Some research directions may eventually develop into independent open-source projects.

Participation in research or open-source work does not automatically imply employment, ownership, equity, compensation, or rights to future commercial projects.

Any commercial collaboration or compensation arrangement should be established separately and explicitly by the people involved.
