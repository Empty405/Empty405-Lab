# Research Scope

## Main question

Do German digital public-service workflows provide sufficient:

- confidentiality
- integrity
- availability
- traceability
- accountability
- accessibility
- transparency
- non-discriminatory access

when information and administrative responsibility move between systems,
departments and employees?

## Initial systems

- jobcenter.digital
- Jobcenter web services
- Jobcenter mobile application
- Bundesagentur für Arbeit services
- official communication channels
- authentication and account workflows
- document and message routing
- administrative record handling

## Out of scope without explicit authorization

- unauthorized account access
- credential attacks
- bypassing authentication or authorization
- accessing third-party personal data
- destructive testing
- denial-of-service testing
- persistence in government infrastructure
