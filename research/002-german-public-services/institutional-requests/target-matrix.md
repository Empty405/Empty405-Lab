# Institutional Request Target Matrix

## Purpose

Route research questions to the institution most likely to have authoritative
knowledge of the relevant process.

---

## TARGET-001 — Bundesagentur für Arbeit / central service

Best suited for:

- architecture of jobcenter.digital
- Postfachservice
- central online account / portal behavior
- Jobcenter-App
- general technical process documentation
- relationship between portal and internal BA systems

Recommended requests:

- REQ-001 questions 1–3
- REQ-001 questions 7–10

Priority:
HIGH

---

## TARGET-002 — BA Datenschutzbeauftragter

Best suited for:

- processing of personal data
- retention periods
- deletion
- access rights
- internal vs online data stores
- auditability from a data-protection perspective
- data subject access

Recommended requests:

- REQ-001 questions 2–3
- REQ-001 questions 7–9

Priority:
HIGH

---

## TARGET-003 — Local Jobcenter / Arbeitsvermittlung

Best suited for:

- practical access to historical communication
- organizational responsibility changes
- visibility of previous records
- team changes
- Vermittlung workflow

Recommended requests:

- REQ-001 questions 4–6
- practical examples of Zuständigkeitswechsel

Priority:
HIGH

## TARGET-004 — Local Jobcenter / Leistungsbereich

- REQ-002 questions 4–6

HIGH

## TARGET-005 — Local Jobcenter management / Geschäftsführung

MEDIUM
