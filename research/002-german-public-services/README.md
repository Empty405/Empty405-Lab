# German Public Services — Security, Integrity & Access Research

Independent open-source research into the security, integrity, accessibility,
data governance and administrative workflows of German digital public services.

## Initial scope

The first research branch focuses on services used in the context of:

- Jobcenter and Bundesagentur für Arbeit
- jobcenter.digital
- web and mobile account workflows
- administrative communication and document routing
- eAkte / internal record flows where publicly documentable
- transfers between departments and responsible offices
- language and accessibility barriers
- data protection and auditability
- potential corruption and discrimination risks

## Research principles

This project distinguishes:

1. Observation
2. Hypothesis
3. Reproduced behavior
4. Corroborated result
5. Confirmed finding

A suspected weakness is not described as a vulnerability, corruption,
discrimination or legal violation until sufficient evidence supports that claim.

## Security boundaries

Research is based on:

- public information
- official documentation
- legislation
- public applications and interfaces
- the researcher's own accounts and data
- lawful institutional requests
- reproducible observations within authorized access

No unauthorized access to third-party accounts or protected systems is part of
the research.

Potential security vulnerabilities will be handled through responsible
disclosure before publication of actionable exploitation details.
